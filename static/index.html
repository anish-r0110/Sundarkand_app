Install App on Mobile

📱 For iPhone / iPad (iOS 16.4+)

  1. Open this site in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (box with upward arrow ⎋) at the bottom.
  3. Scroll down and select "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap "Add" in the top right.
  5. Important: Apple requires the app to be installed to your home screen before push notifications can be enabled!

🤖 For Android Devices

  1. Open this site in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots menu (⋮) in the top right.
  3. Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  4. Follow the prompt to install.
